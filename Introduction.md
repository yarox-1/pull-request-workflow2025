# How does a dog change your life?

For centuries dogs are loyal companions of the human race and througout the years they improved the lifes of humans in multiple ways. That's why in the following I will talk about the advantages of getting a dog.  

Dogs (**Canis familiaris**) are domesticated descentants of wolfs. They were the first ever species to be domesticated by humans over 14,000 years ago. Througout those years humans created different breeds of dogs, each with their unique characteristics.
Therefore dogs today perform multiple roles for humans. For example they are hunting companions, used for herding, protection, therapy, aiding disabled people or for assisting police with detecting drugs.  

But still the advantages of dogs in our every day life are not talked about. Therefore I researched for advantages.  

For this research, I tried to answer the question of how a dog can change your life.  

I found out that dogs improve the overall health of their human. Also they can be very helpful in the house. These are the main two advantages found.
The improved health is shown in different aspects of health. First humans with dogs showed a improved mental health. Probably because dogs are good companions and therefore the humans are not lonely anymore. The other advantage found was the improved fitness of dog owner. This is due to the daily walks and playing dogs require  from their human.  
The second main advantage is based in actions dogs can do in the house. For example dogs can help by closing doors for humans. Also dogs are able to open specific fridges and to then get the human something out of the fridge.
